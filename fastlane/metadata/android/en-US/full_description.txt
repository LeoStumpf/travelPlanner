Tripboard plans your trip in four views of the same data – a free canvas, a notes board, a day-by-day schedule and a map – plus a Today agenda for when you are on the road. It works offline, needs no account and keeps everything on your phone.

SCHEDULE
• Places, transport, accommodation (in nights) and activities as layers on a timeline
• Long-press and drag to move; following items move along, fixed ones stay put
• Spots conflicts, free days and nights without a bed – with one-tap fixes
• Ticket details on transports: stations, train or flight number, seat, booking reference
• Optional time zones, so flight durations and order are always right
• Horizontal or vertical layout, zoom down to the hour

MAP
• OpenStreetMap-based map, long-press to add a place
• My location, navigation hand-off to your maps app
• Save the trip's map offline before you leave

TODAY
• Where you are, where you sleep, your next transport and the day's programme
• Tickets and attachments open with one tap
• Works completely offline

NOTES, FILES & CHECKLISTS
• Keep-style notes, links with previews, images and checklists
• Packing lists from templates (basics, beach, hiking, city) or from another trip
• A file area per trip for tickets, bookings and photos, with folders
• Link one file to several items, e.g. one PDF for both flights
• Share links, text, screenshots or files to Tripboard from any app

BUDGET
• Costs per item, per person or total, paid or open
• Your own exchange rates, so the budget works offline

SHARE WITH FELLOW TRAVELLERS
• Itinerary as text, as PDF or as a calendar file (ICS)
• Only planned and booked items are shared – options and ideas stay private

REMINDERS
• Notifications before departures, check-in and check-out
• Choose the lead times yourself

PLAN WITH CLAUDE
• Copy a guide and your plan into the Claude app, let it research
• Apply its answer with a preview of every change, undoable in one step

YOUR DATA
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Search across all trips, notes, tickets and attachments
• Export trips as ZIP backups, automatic daily backups, 30-day trash
• Accessible: screen-reader labels and alternatives to drag gestures
• In English and German
